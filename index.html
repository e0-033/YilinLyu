A little about me

I have an MA in Art and Politics from Goldsmiths (in case you’re wondering what that actually means, it’s about things like activist and protest art, political aesthetics, and political philosophy—and it’s a field I’m really passionate about). I also have a BA in Photography.

I work across different media and love experimenting with new techniques. I do 3D modelling and real-time rendering with Blender, Unreal Engine and Unity, and I’ve also made a few projects using 3D printing, Rhino and CAD (still learning, but getting there). Coming from a photography background, I’m also familiar with Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, CapCut, After Effects—and, oh and Figma.

Anyway, thanks for visiting. Feel free to DM me about anything!
